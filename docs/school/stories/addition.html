Answer key (for grown-ups)

1: A — 7. 4 apples + 3 bananas = 7 fruits.

2: B — 7. 5 + 2 = 7 throws.

3: C — 9. 6 + 3 = 9 balloons.

4: D — 8. 4 + 4 = 8 stars.

5: A — 5. 3 + 2 = 5 cakes.

6: B — 7. 3 + 4 = 7 fruits.

7: C — 5. 2 + 3 = 5 balls.

8: D — 9. 5 + 4 = 9 stickers.

9: A — 5. 3 + 2 = 5 balloons.

10: B — 7. 2 + 5 = 7 fruits.
