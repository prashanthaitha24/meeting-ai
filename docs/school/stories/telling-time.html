Answer key (for grown-ups)

Story 1: B — 4:00. Homework starts at 3:00 and takes 1 hour, so it finishes at 4:00. That is when Krish starts playing.

Story 2: B — 3:00. 2:00 plus 1 hour of baking is 3:00.

Story 3: B — 7:30. 7:00 plus 30 minutes is half past seven — 7:30.

Story 4: A — 8:30. 8:00 plus 30 minutes is 8:30.

Story 5: C — 6:00. 4:00 plus 2 hours is 6:00.

Story 6: B — 4:00. 1:00 plus 3 hours is 4:00.
