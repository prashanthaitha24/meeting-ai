Answer key (for grown-ups)

1: A — 4:00. 3:00 plus 1 hour of homework is 4:00 — that's when the play begins.

2: B — 3:00. 2:00 plus 1 hour of baking is 3:00.

3: C — 7:30. 7:00 plus 30 minutes is half past seven — 7:30.

4: D — 8:30. 8:00 plus 30 minutes is 8:30.

5: A — 6:00. 4:00 plus 2 hours is 6:00.

6: B — 4:00. 1:00 plus 3 hours is 4:00.

7: C — 11:00. 10:00 plus 1 hour is 11:00.

8: D — 4:30. 4:00 plus 30 minutes is 4:30.

9: A — 6:30. 6:00 plus 30 minutes is 6:30.

10: B — 6:00. 5:00 plus 1 hour is 6:00.

11: C — 7:30. 7:00 plus 30 minutes is 7:30.

12: D — 3:30. 3:00 plus 30 minutes is 3:30.
