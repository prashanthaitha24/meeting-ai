Summary

I'm a Principal Lead SRE with 18+ years in DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering. I have built reliability functions from the ground up, as Yahoo Rivals' first Principal DevOps/SRE and now leading the SRE practice for PG&E's Palantir Foundry data platforms. I have led and mentored engineering teams across onsite and offshore locations, introduced SLIs, SLOs and error budgets that tie reliability to business targets, and set the platform standards other teams build on.

My strengths are prioritizing and architecting. Every day starts with one question: what are the three things that matter most today? The same discipline shapes how I design systems: observability built in from day one, cost treated as a design requirement, and security grounded in least privilege. It is how I have migrated systems to AWS with zero downtime, moved a full CDN between providers twice without an outage, and cut deployments from hours to minutes.

I lead from the front and I love being hands-on. I'm as comfortable setting technical direction for a team as I am taking an individual-contributor role and building the infrastructure myself. Learning is a lifelong passion: I'm completing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Penn State (December 2026), which led me to build ThavionAI, a free education platform, and Klaxon, an intelligent on-call platform where incidents arrive already triaged, both using RAG and MCP. Next, I want to lead the infrastructure that runs LLMs, serving them reliably and optimizing them for latency, throughput and cost. Happy to connect.
